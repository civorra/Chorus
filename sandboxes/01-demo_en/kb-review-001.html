§1.1 Scope 001-timber-frame-intro-simul.txt
This document applies to timber-frame buildings up to 2 storeys (ground + first floor), in wind zones 1 to 4 and climate zones A, B, C. Main structural element types: load-bearing studs, non-load-bearing studs, sole plates, top plates, rafters, joists, insulation, vapour control layers.

§1.2 Contractor qualification 001-timber-frame-intro-simul.txt
The contractor must hold a recognised timber frame construction qualification (e.g. UKTFA member or equivalent) before commencing structural works.

§2.2 Moisture content (BS EN 1995-1-1 §2.3) 001-timber-frame-intro-simul.txt
