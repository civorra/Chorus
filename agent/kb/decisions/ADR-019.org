#+TITLE: ADR-019 — CPAN distribution named «Chorus», not «Chorus-Engine»
#+DATE: 2026-11-21
#+STATUS: proposed
#+DOMAIN: cpan
#+ENG:

* Context

The CPAN distribution originally shipped as =Chorus-Engine=, reflecting the
central role of =Chorus::Engine= in the first public release.  As the package
grew to include =Chorus::Expert= and =Chorus::Frame= as first-class modules,
the single-module name became misleading: users searching for the expert-system
toolkit would not naturally find =Chorus-Engine= on CPAN.

At v2.01 the distribution was renamed to =Chorus= to match the umbrella brand
and to signal that the three modules (=Expert=, =Engine=, =Frame=) form a
cohesive system rather than a single-purpose library.

* Decision

The CPAN distribution name is =Chorus= (=Makefile.PL=: =NAME => 'Chorus'=).
The three modules retain their individual namespaces (=Chorus::Expert=,
=Chorus::Engine=, =Chorus::Frame=).
Never revert to =Chorus-Engine= and do not introduce =Chorus-Frame= or
=Chorus-Expert= as separate distributions.

* Positive consequences

- Consistent branding: the distribution name matches the module prefix.
- Simpler =cpanm= invocation: =cpanm Chorus= installs all three modules.
- Future modules (e.g. =Chorus::Planner= — ADR-020) can be added to the
  same distribution without a renaming event.

* Negative consequences / accepted trade-offs

- =Chorus-Engine= links on rt.cpan.org and old documentation point to the
  old name; both queues must be monitored during the transition period.
- The name =Chorus= is generic; namespace collision risk on CPAN is low
  but non-zero.

* Rejected alternatives

| Alternative | Reason for rejection |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Keep =Chorus-Engine= | Misleading once Expert and Frame became first-class; confuses users looking for the toolkit |
| Separate dists per module (=Chorus-Expert=, =Chorus-Engine=, =Chorus-Frame=) | Unnecessary split; the three modules are always used together; version synchronisation burden |
